fastget privacy policy

Last updated: 2026

fastget is a download manager made of a browser extension and a local app that runs on your own computer. It is designed so that your data stays on your machine.

What the extension accesses

  • Downloads: when a download starts, the extension reads its URL, suggested file name, size and the referring page so fastget can fetch the same file.
  • Website cookies (optional): only if you turn it on, and only so downloads that require a login or membership keep working. Cookies for the download's own site are sent to the local fastget app for that one download. You can leave this off; public downloads work without it.
  • Local storage: your settings (number of connections, speed limit, whether to ask each time) are stored on your device.

Where your data goes

All of the above is sent only to the fastget app running on the same computer, over your computer's internal loopback connection. The extension and app do not send your URLs, cookies, file names, or browsing activity to the developer or any third party. There are no analytics or trackers.

The fastget app connects to the download server you chose (the file's own website) to fetch the file, exactly as your browser would. It connects to no other servers, except to check for app updates if you ask it to.

Data retention

The download list and your settings are stored on your computer until you remove a download or uninstall the app. Nothing is kept by the developer.
